# Q&A question bank

What the daily Q&A refill (runbook §11, step B4) draws from once follow-ups and data-driven cards are used up. Never re-ask an answered or skipped question to the same person within 90 days. Check the Profile tab and Review first. Write cards per the card guide in `docs/system.md` (Q&A tab): Yes/No when a yes answers it, 1–3 word options, the likely answer first, ids like `(iNNN)` / `(mNN)` at the end.

## Everyone
- **Dinner votes:** every dinner in Meals the person hasn't voted on (`Dinner vote: how do you feel about <name>? (mNN) Options: 😍; 🙂; 😐; 👎`). New meals first.
- **Breakfast and lunch votes:** same emoji card for breakfasts, lunches and snacks once dinners are covered ("Breakfast vote: …").
- **Favorites:** favorite fruit, snack, cereal, drink, pizza topping, taco style, dessert, ice cream.
- **Won't-eats and spice:** foods you won't eat; spice level; foods you'd like to try.
- **Wishes:** one dinner / breakfast / snack you wish we made more often.
- **Product picks:** for items we buy in more than one flavor or brand, which one (FruiChia, Siggi's, granola bars, chips, cereal).

## Kids (kid-sized: emoji, Yes/No, 2–4 picks)
- School lunch: best thing, worst thing, Lunchables yes/no and which kind, sandwich or no sandwich.
- After-school and practice snacks: rate the snacks we buy (emoji), what you want after practice.
- Breakfast on school days: what you actually eat, how much time you have.
- Never ask kids things that change what's bought; their answers go to item notes and the Profile tab.

## Parents
- **Swap rules** (`ok_swap`) for every item with a blank: "If <item> is out, OK to swap? (iNNN) Options: Yes; No".
- **Shelf life** for perishables without one yet (berries, greens, deli, dairy, bread, meat once thawed).
- **Size and quantity** for things that go to waste (smaller size? fewer?).
- **Meal details** for meals with missing ingredients or cut-off names (the parent who plans meals).
- **Household rhythm:** who places which order, which weekend for the monthly Target run, busy nights (sports) that need quick dinners, leftover nights, guests and holidays (event weeks).
- **Keep it?** for meals rated low or not made in 60+ days (after Plan Archive has history).

## Seasonal (re-ask each season)
- Grilling season yes/no, soup season favorites, holiday dinners and baking, summer snacks.
