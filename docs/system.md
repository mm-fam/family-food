# Poppy 2.0 system reference

The app is named **Poppy 2.0** (the family's name for its earlier AI project). The repo and URL keep the `family-food` name so existing home-screen links keep working.

_Last updated: Nov 1 2026 (api 12: Cart Jobs, so a parent's phone can start a cart build or check; api 11: Products tab, In-cart tag, aisles, Amazon store)_

## Purpose
A shared phone app where anyone in the family adds what we need, picks dinners, and taps staples, with Claude behind it closing the loop from receipts and filling store carts. Design goal from day one: **it must never become a chore or go stale.** Automation (receipts, "probably due", draft lists) does the upkeep, not the family.

## People and roles
| Who | Role |
|---|---|
| Mom | Runs household food. Final say on menus, carts, what's bought. |
| Mike | Builder and admin. Owns the repo, Sheet, Apps Script deployment, Chrome sessions. |
| Kids | App users. Adds land as `requested`; a parent approves. |
| Claude | Operator: receipt ingest, cart building, catalog upkeep, Review questions, code changes. |

## Stores
- **Giant Eagle (GE):** kid lunches, snacks, everyday dinners. Curbside/delivery. myPerks rewards account.
- **Whole Foods (WF):** ordered through Amazon. Premium items, produce, specialty (Nounos yogurt, Creminelli salami, brie bites, pizza dough).
- **Target (TGT):** toiletries, personal care, paper and household. Drive Up / pickup about once a month (online orders also ship). Mostly non-food.
- **Amazon (AMZ):** fourth store (app plumbing since api 11; cart building not started). Like Target, never part of `Either`.
- `Either` = either grocery store (GE or WF), whichever order is next. Never Target or Amazon.

### App behavior
- Four tabs, evenly spaced: **List, Shop, Meals, Inbox** (Inbox was Q&A, before that Ask; its internal id stays `ask`). **List** shows a count of active items (green; orange when a parent has kids' requests waiting); **Inbox** shows open cards for you plus, for parents, approvals waiting. First launch asks passcode + "Who's this?" (stored on the device). While the first state loads (after Unlock, and on every return visit) a loading screen shows the logo, a spinner and "Checking the passcode…" / "Getting the list…", adds a "server is waking up" line after 4 s, and offers **Try again** if the load fails (a wrong saved passcode goes back to the passcode form).
- **List** = what we need, as **one list by aisle** (Nov 1 family demo: timing matters more than the store; wherever we shop, we grab what we can). Kids' requests on top, then **filter pills All · 🍽 For meals · 🧺 Stock** (Nov 1; a row is for a meal if it was added for one (`meal_id`) or its item is in a meal planned in the next 7 days, tagged "🍽 Tue · <meal>"; Stock = everything else; starts on All), then one accordion per aisle (Items.group) with a one-line peek when folded. Rows show a small store dot (where we usually get it), an **Only at <store>** tag when Items.store_only = Y, qty, note, a meal tag (🍽 meal name) and who asked (`wanted_by`; yellow when 2+). **Everything starts folded each time the app opens**; what you open stays open until the app is closed. A row that leaves the List (we have it, bought, taken off) stays in place crossed out for ~6 s with **Undo**. A dismissible **Put Poppy on your Home Screen** card shows when the app is opened in a browser instead of from the home screen. Next week is a folded card. No check-off at home: tapping a row opens the item screen.
- **🛒 In store** (List header; everyone incl. kids): pick a store (GE / WF / Target / All); rows by aisle with checkboxes. Every row shows except ones **only** another store carries; rows usually bought elsewhere say "usually <store>". **A virtual cart** (Nov 1): tapping a row puts it in the cart: the row is `bought` at once (note "in the cart in store (<name>)") and leaves the list for good; no crossed-out rows and no taking it back out there. A folded **🛒 In the cart** card at the bottom lists what's in the cart today, for everyone shopping together; "N in the cart · N to go" on top. Something put in by mistake is added again from Shop. Done leaves the mode.
- **Shop** = where things get added. Search box (no match → "Add … as something new"), smart filter pills with counts (Probably due, For dinners = ingredients of dinners planned in the next 14 days, Usuals = Always or 2+ orders, Running low = low/out Stock sighting in 14 days, Bought lately, On the list; empty pills hide) and store pills. Rows by aisle with a + button (✓ when on the list), store dot, last bought, latest at-home sighting, and (Nov 1) **why it left the List lately** when it isn't on it: the last finished List row in the past week ("🛒 In the cart today (Joni)", "✓ Bought yesterday", "🏠 We had some · 2 days ago (Mike)", "Taken off the List · …"). Aisles are accordions that start folded (peek of names, Open all / Close all); typing a search opens every aisle it matches.
- **Item screen** (from List or Shop, same layout): name, store, aisle; status (on the list / asked by / meal tag) with qty stepper and a trip note, or a big Add button; one-tap "Have some at home?" Plenty / Some / A little / Count (on the List: Plenty / Some / a count close the row as `have`, **A little keeps it on the List** and only logs the sighting; off the List: just a Stock sighting via `stockLog`); quiet Next week / Bought it / Take off; **History** (orders, last bought, typical gap, last 6 purchases with store and price, at-home sightings) and **What we know** (Items notes + answered Ask questions that mention the item), loaded with `itemInfo`; folded **Details** (store we use, **can other stores get it?** (store_only, api 9), swap OK, how often, brand/size, aisle, notes; parents edit, kids read).
- **Meals tab:** dinner plan as two swipe-sideways **week strips** (about 2½ days visible on a phone): **This week** (today → Saturday) on top, **Next week** (Sun → Sat) below, folded until tapped. Each day tile: tap the meal name to pick a dinner (phone's own picker), **Ingredients** opens what's needed under that week's strip. Plan data is still the rolling 14 days; nights past next Saturday are planned from the meal card; **Meal ideas** (what people pasted and where each stands); **Our meals** library. Each meal row carries **stat tags**: family votes (emoji counts, "Favorite" when 2+ votes average ≥ 3.5), Tonight / Next <day>, Made N× · last <date>, cost tier + estimate ($ < $15, $$ < $30, $$$; shown when most ingredients have a receipt price, "+" when some are missing), New. Sort pills: A–Z, Favorites, Cheapest, Not lately. Tapping a meal opens the **meal card**: day chips for this week and next (tap = plan it, tap again = off; a night with another meal gets swapped, with Undo), ingredients with "Add N to the List", notes, Edit meal. **💡 Idea**: paste a recipe, link or idea; saved to Meal Ideas for Claude to turn into a meal (kids' ideas go to a parent first). Stats and ideas need backend api 7. **Meal slots (api 8):** each day tile also lists 🥣 breakfast / 🥪 lunch / 🍎 snack; tapping the day opens a **day sheet** with all four slots (picker per slot, ↗ to the meal card). Meals have a kind (kind pills in Our meals; "Usually for" in Edit meal); a meal card plans into its own slot. Leftover rows show as "Leftover <meal>". **Day tiles (Nov 1):** Today / day + date, then Breakfast, Lunch, Snack, Dinner top to bottom at the same size; tapping the tile opens the day sheet; "Dinner ingredients" opens under the strip. **+ New is gone** (💡 Meal idea covers new meals; + New shows only on backends without ideas). **Kids (api 9)** don't plan: the meal card shows **Want this?** (slot + This week / Next week / Anytime → **Ask for it**, action `mealRequest`), and the day sheet is read-only. Parents see **Meal requests** at the top of Meals with **Plan it** (opens the meal card; planning a day answers the request) and **Not now**. **→ Hearth** (parents): builds a paste-ready message for the Hearth display's AI assistant (Hearth Helper) with this or next week's Breakfast / Lunch / Snack / Dinner, Copy / Share. See `docs/hearth.md`.
- **💡 in the header** (everyone, api 9): "Idea for Poppy", free text about the app → `feedback` → App Feedback tab; the morning check reports new rows to the owner. It links to the meal-idea sheet for meal ideas.
- **Admin screen** (gear, only names in Settings.admins (comma-separated; both parents today), else the first parent): counts per store; per store **Build the cart** and **Check the cart** (api 12) with the latest job's status line (waiting / Claude is working on it / Cart built 5:02 pm: 18 items, $142 / Couldn't … ) and Cancel for a waiting job. On older backends: "Ask Claude to build the cart" (sets `Settings.order_requested`).
- **Cart heads-up on the List** (api 12): one line at the top while a cart job is waiting or running, and for 12 hours after a build finishes ("Giant Eagle cart is ready: … Review it and check out").
- **Inbox tab** (was Q&A, before that Ask; Nov 1): parents first see **Approvals** (kids' List requests and meal requests, also still on List and Meals). Then a deck **grouped into sections, most important first**: kids ⏰ Due soon → 🍽 How was it? → 🔎 Missions → 💬 Quick questions; parents ⚖ Decisions → ⏰ Due soon → 🍽 → 🔎 → 💬. Section chips with counts narrow the deck to one section. The section comes from a Review `section` column when present, else from the card: type `task`, "due <day>" or the morning check's "School lunches for next week" card → Due soon; `rate` / `still` → How was it?; `check` → Missions; for parents, `clarify` / `swap` / `miss` / `unknown_code` or a `preference` about an item or idea (or "Stop buying", "Add it to Our meals") → Decisions; the rest → Quick questions. **Points (api 13, Inbox step 2):** a scoreboard on top of the Inbox shows your points today against the daily goal (a ring; Settings `points_goal`, default 10), your streak (days in a row at the goal; an unfinished today doesn't break it) and a **family leaderboard** of this week's points (Mon–Sun) with streaks and a 👑 on last week's winner. Each card shows what it's worth: Due soon 5 (2 after its `due_at`), Mission 3, How was it? / Still eating? 2, everything else 1, Don't care 0. **Kitchen help** (parents' section 🧑‍🍳): every planned dinner makes one "Did <kid> help cook <meal> yesterday?" card per kid (type `helped`, audience parents, Review `points_to` = the kid; Cooked it / Helped / Not this time); the answer records what they did and gives the kid 10 / 5 / 0 points; unanswered ones close after 3 days. **Reconciling the List is missions only (api 21; Mike):** tapping "Have some at home already?" on the List earns no points. A kid's Plenty / Some there, and a kid's "It's here" on a Did we get it? card, never take a row off by themselves: the sighting is logged and the parents get a `have_check` card in Decisions ("<kid> says we have some of <item> at home. Take it off the List? (Lnnnnn) Options: Yes, take it off; No, keep it"); Yes sets the row `have`; answering scores the parent `approve` (1). Kids see their Did we get it? cards as 🔎 Missions (3 points on answer). **Scoring start (api 18):** Settings `points_start` (yyyy-mm-dd) is the first day that counts; anything scored earlier is ignored by the scoreboard, streaks, leaderboard, 👑 and History (the Review/Points rows stay as a record). Before that day the scoreboard shows "🏁 Scoring starts tomorrow" and cards and toasts show no points. Set to 2026-11-02 so everyone starts even. **Points for helping plan and shop (api 16):** adding to the List 1 (parents when they add, or when they join someone else's row; a kid's add scores when a parent approves it; up to 5 a day), asking for a meal 2 (up to 2 a day), a parent handling a kid's request 1 (Approve or Skip on a List request, Plan it or Not now on a meal request). Not scored: a meal's ingredients added at once (`addMeal`), List adds made by a mission answer, a kid cancelling their own meal request. These go to the **Points** tab. **How points work + Points history (api 14):** the scoreboard has two links that open one sheet with two tabs. *How it works* lists every way to score (from `state.points.rules`, the backend's `POINT_RULES`), what earns nothing (Don't care, Pass, Later, meal requests, approvals, unanswered Kitchen help), the daily goal, streaks, the Mon–Sun leaderboard and the 👑 (ties crown everyone tied). *History* (`pointsLog`, read-only) shows weekly totals with goal days and winners, then every scored answer newest first: who got the points, how many, the rule, the card and answer, the time, and who answered when it was someone else; filter by person. **Missions (api 15, Inbox step 3):** on the first app open each day the backend gives each kid up to 3 "go check" cards (type `check`, section 🔎 Missions, 3 points): "Go check the <item>: how much do we have? It's for <meal>." Picks, split so each kid gets different ones: ingredients of meals planned today + the next 2 days first, then the most overdue "probably due" items; never something on the List, sighted at home in the last 3 days, or already on an open check. The card has Plenty / Some / A little / Out plus "Or count them". The answer logs a Stock row right away (source `mission`, so "probably due" updates live); A little, Out or 0 also puts the item on the List (a kid's answer as `requested`, a parent's as `needed`, note "Mission: …"). Unanswered missions close after 2 days. **Did we get it? (api 17):** settles List rows no receipt will close. On the first app open each day the backend makes section 🧾 cards (3 points): (1) a store cart filled 2+ days ago with 3+ rows → one "Did the <store> order come?" card to the parents (type `settle_order`; Yes, all of it → those rows `bought`; Some of it → one card per row; Not yet → asked again in 5 days); (2) `needed` rows whose item someone saw at home (Plenty / Some / a count) after the row was added, then `needed` rows untouched 7 days, as "Did we get the <item>? Go look" cards, up to 2 per kid (It's here → `have`, or `bought` if it was carted, + Stock some; Not here → stays, Stock out); (3) the rest, and carted rows of smaller carts, to the parents, up to 4 a day (Got it → `bought`; Still need it → stays; Don't need it → `skipped`). Never held rows, kids' requests, or a row asked about in the last 5 days. **Meals that left the plan (api 19):** a List row's meal tag only counts while that meal is planned (2 days back through the plan window; rows added today keep it); otherwise the row shows as Stock with no tag. Each day, `needed` rows still tied to an unplanned meal (added 2+ days ago) get one parents' card per meal (type `settle_meal`, "<meal> isn't on the plan anymore, but N things for it are still on the List… Take them off / Keep them"): Take them off → those rows `skipped`; Keep them → `meal_id` cleared, rows stay as regular items. Unanswered cards close after 3 days; a card whose row was settled another way (receipt, In store) disappears from the deck. Kids' order: Due soon → Did we get it? → How was it? → Missions → Quick questions; parents: Decisions → Due soon → Did we get it? → Kitchen help → … The deck is a deck of cards, one question at a time, with progress. The control comes from the question's `Options:` text: `Yes; No` → big Yes/No buttons, swipe right = yes, left = no; 2–4 options → big tap buttons; 5+ → dropdown; short emoji-only options → emoji row; no options → text box. `Other (type it)` opens a text box; `Don't care` = skip. Every card has "Something else? Type it", "Don't care", and "Later" (moves it to the back; swipe left on non-yes/no cards). **Since api 10 Later is saved** (`reviewLater` → Review `later_count` + `later_at`): cards put off stay at the back across days, oldest Later first, and the daily Q&A loop retires a card after 3 Laters (runbook §11). Item, meal, idea and review ids like (i108), (m16), (x001), (r016) are hidden on screen. Blank/absent `audience` = parents; kids see "All caught up" until per-person questions exist. Answers are saved as `<answer> (<name>)`.
- Polls state every 20 s. Every write returns fresh state. A state read that started before or during a write is ignored, so an approved request no longer flashes back.
- Kids (names in Settings.kids) add as `requested`; parents approve.
- "Ask Claude to build the cart" (admin screen) only sets `Settings.order_requested`.

### Backend API (`action` in POST body, plus `pass`)
`ping`, `state`, `add`, `update`, `addMeal`, `planSet`, `mealSave`, `itemSave`, `reviewAnswer`, `reviewPass`, `listHave`, `listHold`, `requestOrder`, `itemInfo`, `stockLog`, `mealIdea`, `mealRequest`, `feedback`, `reviewLater` {id}, `cartJob` {store, kind}, `cartJobCancel` {id}, plus `version` (no passcode; returns only `api`). `state` includes `api` (backend version; 2 = reviewPass + Review audience; 3 = listHave/listHold + Stock; 4 = List `wanted_by`, Settings.admins; 5 = `itemInfo` {item_id} → purchases/stock/answers (no state returned), `stockLog` {item_id, level}, `state.stock` = latest sighting per item; 6 = Plan by date: `state.plan` = rows dated today … today+13 (`planDays` 14), `planSet` {date, meal_id} (older apps' week_of + day still work); 7 = `mealIdea` {text} → Meal Ideas row, `state.ideas` (open ideas + ones finished in the last 14 days), `state.mealStats` per meal {voters, votes, score, made, last, next, cost, priced, linked} computed from Review votes, Plan + Plan Archive and Receipts; 8 = meal slots; **9** = Items `store_only` via `itemSave`, `mealRequest` {meal_id, slot, when} → Review `meal_request` row (audience parents, `asked_by`), `feedback` {text, tab} → App Feedback row, kids can't `planSet` / `mealSave`, daily automatic Q&A cards (below), `rate` cards count as votes in mealStats; **10** = `reviewLater`; **11** = `state.products` = the exact product per item per store from the Products tab ({item_id: {GE: {name, size, aisle}}}, best row: exact > likely > alt, then newest), and List rows may carry `cart` + `cart_at`; **12** = `cartJob` {store: GE/WF/TGT/AMZ, kind: build/check} → a `queued` Cart Jobs row (parents only; asking again while one is open returns it), `cartJobCancel` {id} for a queued/waiting job, `state.cartJobs` = open jobs + the last 14 days, newest first).

**Automatic Q&A cards (api 9)**, made in `getState` the first time anyone opens the app each day (Settings `cards_through`, no trigger needed): **How was it?** (type `rate`, one per person) the day after a dinner or breakfast that's still new to us (made ≤ 2 times before), max 2 per person per day, catching up at most 3 days; unanswered ones close after 5 days. **Still eating …?** (type `still`, Yes/No) weekly (Settings `still_through`): 2 per person from food items bought 2+ times, not asked to that person in 90 days. `add` on an item already on the list adds the asker to `wanted_by` instead of bumping qty and `held` (rows moved to next week). The app hides features whose backend isn't deployed yet. Writes run under a script lock. GET works too for a quick browser check: `/exec?action=ping&pass=...`.

**"Probably due"** (computed in `getState`): item not Drop, has `avg_days` and `last_bought`, not already on the list, and days since last buy ≥ `avg_days − 2`. Top 12 by how overdue.

**Nightly trigger** (`nightly`, ~3 am, installed by `setup()`): moves bought/skipped/have List rows older than 14 days to **List Archive**, and Plan rows dated more than 14 days ago to **Plan Archive** (no `setup()` rerun needed).

Types: clarify / preference / vote / profile, plus **rate** (How was it?), **still** (Still eating?), **meal_request** (a kid's meal ask; shown on the Meals tab, not in Q&A). `audience` = who sees it in Ask (a name, `parents` or `all`; blank = parents). **Pass ↗** on a card sets audience to another family member and `passed_by` to the passer; their card shows "<name> passed this to you". ## Claude's jobs (details in runbooks)
1. **Receipt ingest:** new GE/WF receipts → Receipts rows → match by aliases → active List rows to `bought` → unknowns to Review.
2. **Cart building:** when a Cart Jobs `build` job is queued (or older `order_requested` is set), fill that store's cart in Chrome from List rows, picking each product from Products (added from the store's past purchases), respect ok_swap, report swaps/misses to Review, set rows `carted` + `cart`. Never check out. Needs the owner's computer awake with Chrome open and the store signed in (runbook §2, §12).
2b. **Cart check** (runbook §12): read a store's online cart and sync it to the List (`carted` + `cart`), so the app shows what's already in the cart.
3. **Catalog upkeep:** new items get next free id + J:M formulas. Products upkeep from the stores' purchase history (runbook §2).
